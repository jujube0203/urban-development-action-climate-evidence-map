`input_sample.csv` is a blank input template. `city_attributes.xlsx` contains attributes assembled from external city data sources. The full dataset includes bibliographic records, such as article titles and abstracts, together with externally sourced attributes. These materials remain subject to the rights, terms of use, and attribution requirements of their original publishers and data providers.

Reuse or redistribution of these data should therefore follow the applicable source-specific terms. The repository license does not grant permissions held by third-party rights holders. Provenance and required citations for externally sourced data should be retained when the dataset is reused.
